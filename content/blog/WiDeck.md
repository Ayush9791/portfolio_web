## The idea

I always wanted a stream deck thought it would look cool for my development setup, but boy oh boy that thing cost a lot. So I thought why don't I make
one instead.

## The Project

Wi-Deck combines an Android app with a Windows desktop application, connected over LAN.

## How It Works

Connect your phone and PC to the same network to launch up to 5 apps and control your desktop volume directly from your phone.

## What's Next?

iOS and Linux support, plus more features. Let's see where this goes!

## Takeaway

Why spend a fortune on a tool when you can build your own?
